# Concept Overview

## What it is

A B2B supply-chain app connecting dark kitchens with fresh produce from Mexico City's Central de Abasto, one of the largest wholesale food markets in the world.

## Core capabilities

- **Ordering** — dark kitchens place orders directly through the app, replacing manual/phone-based purchasing from the Central de Abasto
- **Quality control** — real-time analysis of product quality at the point of sourcing or delivery
- **Delivery** — logistics from Central de Abasto to each dark kitchen
- **BNPL financing** — integration with financing partners so dark kitchens can pay on a "buy now, pay later" basis, addressing a common cash-flow constraint in the food-service sector

## What this venture will require once development starts

- Ordering/marketplace platform connecting buyers (dark kitchens) and suppliers
- A quality-control process — likely a mix of manual inspection and computer-vision-assisted checks
- Delivery/logistics integration or partnership
- A BNPL fintech partner and the underlying credit/risk integration

## My role as PMO

Scoping the venture: defining the ordering and quality-control model, and identifying delivery and BNPL financing partners ahead of development kickoff.
